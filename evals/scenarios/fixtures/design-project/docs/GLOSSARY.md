# Glossary

One canonical name per concept. Check here before naming anything; update this file in the same change as any new or renamed term. Rules: [`CONVENTIONS.md`](CONVENTIONS.md) §8. Definitions say what a thing is or does; the owner says how it behaves.

## Combat

| Term | Means | Owner | Not to be confused with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Arrow** | The ammunition a bow fires. | [Archery](design/combat/archery.md) | Bolt |
| **Hunting bow** | The basic bow the player starts with. | [Archery](design/combat/archery.md) | Longbow |

## Survival

| Term | Means | Owner | Not to be confused with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stamina** | The pool that sprinting and other hard effort draw on. | [Stamina](design/survival/stamina.md) | Health |
