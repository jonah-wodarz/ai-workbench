# Stamina
> The budget that limits how long the player can act at full effort.

## Intent
Stamina makes sustained effort a cost. It serves VIS-P1 (scarcity): sprinting, swimming and fighting draw on the same limited pool.

## Rules
### Pool
- **STA-R1** Sprinting drains stamina. `locked`

## Open questions
- **STA-Q1** Does drawing a bow drain stamina? `major`

## Working notes
Swimming and climbing may share the pool with sprinting.
