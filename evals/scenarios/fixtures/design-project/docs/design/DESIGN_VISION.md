# Design Vision
> Hollowreach is a first-person survival game in which scarcity turns every action into a choice.

## Pillars
- **VIS-P1** Scarcity: every resource is finite, and spending it is a decision the player feels.
- **VIS-P2** Readable danger: the player can always tell why they were hurt.
- **VIS-P3** Quiet tension: silence and distance are tools, not filler.

## Scope
A single-player wilderness survival game. Multiplayer, base building and a story campaign are out of scope.

## Systems
- **Archery** ([`combat/archery.md`](combat/archery.md)): the main way to fight at range, built around scarce arrows.
- **Stamina** ([`survival/stamina.md`](survival/stamina.md)): the budget that limits how long the player can act at full effort.
