### D-LMP-001 — The burn level is fixed for the night
2026-11-10 · accepted · rules LMP-R1
**Decision:** One choice at dusk, no adjustment afterwards.
**Why:** A single commitment keeps the night short and the choice legible.
**Rejected:** Adjusting the lamp every hour — more control, but the night becomes bookkeeping.
