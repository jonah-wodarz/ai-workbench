# Design Vision
> Players keep a lighthouse through a season of storms. Every night asks what to spend the scarce oil on.

## Pillars
- **VIS-P1** The weather is the opponent; players never compete with each other.
- **VIS-P2** Every loss can be traced to a choice the players made that night.

## Scope
In scope: one lighthouse, one season, two to four players. Out of scope: a campaign across seasons.

## Systems
- [Weather](world/weather.md) sets how hard a night is and tells the players in advance.
- [Lamp](keeper/lamp.md) turns oil into reach, the players' only tool.
- [Ships](world/ships.md) are what the players protect and how a night is scored.
